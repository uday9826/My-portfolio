A little about me

Hi, I'm Uday Yadav — a B.Tech (CSE, specializing in AI & ML) student at Sagar Group of Institutions, Bhopal, currently in my 5th semester. I enjoy turning ideas into working software, from ML-driven web platforms to everyday full-stack builds.

My most recent project, the UPI Offline Mesh is a demonstration project for offline payment communication using a Bluetooth-style mesh network. The system simulates a situation where the sender has no internet connection. A payment is encrypted on the sender side and passed between nearby virtual devices until a bridge device with internet connectivity uploads the payment to the Spring Boot backend. The backend then decrypts, validates, prevents duplicate transactions, and settles the payment

Outside of tech, I'm into Japanese storytelling and anime, PC gaming, and staying consistent at the gym.
